Safe Circle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Safe Circle app ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. Your privacy matters to us, and the App is designed to keep your data on your own device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Safe Circle does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not use the internet to send your data anywhere, and does not contain advertising or analytics tools.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App stores the following information only in a local database on your device:

  • Names, phone numbers, and relationship labels of trusted contacts that you add manually.
  • Your safety check-in history (status, optional note, date and time).
  • Your app settings, such as your custom SOS message.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it.

3. Permissions

Safe Circle does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive permission. When you tap Call or Message, the App simply opens your phone's dialer or messaging app with the number or text prefilled. You choose whether to proceed.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or social login providers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual contacts or check-ins inside the App, use the "Clear all data" option in Settings, or uninstall the App to remove all stored data permanently.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children. Parents and guardians may use the App to keep a family circle of trusted contacts.

7. Important Safety Notice

Safe Circle is a personal convenience tool. It does not replace official emergency services. In a real emergency, always contact your local police, ambulance, or emergency number directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published within the App listing and take effect when posted. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Your Consent

By using Safe Circle, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
